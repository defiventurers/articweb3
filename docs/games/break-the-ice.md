# Break the Ice

## Deployed ruleset

- **Game ID:** `break-the-ice`
- **Ruleset version:** `panchi-vasantha-2006-1.0.0`
- **Traditional game:** Panchi
- **Region:** Mysore, Karnataka, India
- **Evidence status:** Complete named ruleset with one declared digital table policy
- **Source basis:** R. Vasantha, *Maharaja’s Games and Puzzles* (2006), structured Panchi rules summary

Break the Ice publishes the named Vasantha Panchi ruleset. It does not merge capture-gated finishing rules or cowrie tables from other South Asian race-game traditions.

## Heritage rules implemented

- Two players use five pieces each.
- Seven cowries generate the movement value; the number of mouths up is the value.
- Zero mouths up loses the throw.
- A throw of 1, 5 or 7 may enter one waiting piece.
- A throw of 1, 5 or 7 grants another throw after the selected movement is completed.
- Each player starts at the opposite end of an eleven-space bottom track.
- The route reaches the centre, climbs a five-space stem, enters a square circuit, completes the circuit, and climbs the remaining five inner spaces.
- One selected piece moves the complete throw value.
- Exact landing on an unprotected opposing piece sends it back off-board.
- Marked protected spaces prevent capture.
- Leaving beyond the final marked space requires the exact remaining value.
- The first player to finish all five pieces wins.
- No capture is required before entering the square circuit or final inner route in this named ruleset.

## Declared digital table policy

The source summary does not fully specify stacking and blocking. This version freezes the following policy rather than silently borrowing it from another game:

- A board space holds at most one piece.
- A friendly occupied space blocks landing.
- An opposing piece on a protected space blocks landing.
- Pieces may pass over occupied spaces.
- An opposing piece on an unprotected destination is captured by exact landing.

Changing this policy requires a new ruleset version.

## Route engine

The engine stores a separate route array for each player. Both routes contain fifty-six playable progress positions:

1. six bottom-track positions from that player's start to the centre;
2. five shared stem positions;
3. forty circuit positions, traversed in opposite directions;
4. five final inner-path positions;
5. exact exit at progress fifty-six.

Physical shared spaces use common IDs even when players reach them at different progress values. Single-occupancy validation therefore applies across both routes.

## Cowrie and replay guarantees

- Online cowrie faces are generated by the authoritative server.
- Every throw records seven binary shell faces, the resulting value, bonus status, a nonce and a proof hash.
- The server validates entry, movement, capture, protected landing and exact exit.
- Room state, throw history, final state, audit chain and match proof hash persist with the match.
- Replaying the ordered roll and movement log reproduces the final result.

## Product modes

- Practice against a deterministic Glacier Guide bot.
- Local two-player hotseat.
- Daily Cowrie Drill, clearly labelled as a modern one-turn decision puzzle.
- Public and private server-authoritative online rooms.
- Reconnectable persisted matches.
- Break-the-Ice-filtered match history.

## Safety boundary

Break the Ice is free-play only. It has no entry locking, wagering, payout or token reward. Wallets provide optional platform identity and persistent history only.
