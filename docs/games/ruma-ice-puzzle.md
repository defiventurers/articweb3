# Ruma Ice Puzzle

**Ruleset:** Tchuka Ruma relay-sowing solitaire  
**Players:** Solo  
**Ruleset ID:** `tchuka-ruma-puzzle-0.1.0`

Four ordinary pits begin with two counters each. Sow rightward through the Ruma store and wrap to the first pit. An occupied ordinary landing relays automatically. Landing in the Ruma allows another choice. Landing in an empty ordinary pit fails. Move all eight counters into the Ruma to win.

The puzzle rules are documented, but the exact Indian living-tradition provenance is disputed. The product must not market this ruleset as unquestionably ancient.
