# Ruma Ice Puzzle

## Ruleset

- Product: Ruma Ice Puzzle
- Game name used in the mathematical literature: Tchuka Ruma
- Ruleset: `tchuka-ruma-campbell-chavey-1995-2x4-1.0.0`
- Baseline: Paul J. Campbell and Darrah P. Chavey, “Tchuka Ruma Solitaire,” *The UMAP Journal* 16.4 (1995)
- Board: four ordinary pits plus one Ruma
- Opening: two counters in each ordinary pit, eight counters total

## Rules

Choose a non-empty ordinary pit and sow its counters one by one toward the Ruma. If counters remain after reaching the Ruma, wrap to the first ordinary pit and continue.

- Last counter in the Ruma: the move ends safely and the player may choose any non-empty pit next.
- Last counter in a non-empty ordinary pit: lift that pit and continue the same move.
- Last counter in an empty ordinary pit: the puzzle is lost.
- All eight counters in the Ruma: the puzzle is won.

The included solver verifies the classic six-move solution and powers optional guided practice.

## Provenance boundary

The rules are well documented as a mathematical solitaire, but claims about a precise living Indian community tradition are disputed. The product therefore credits the documented Campbell–Chavey / Degrazia rules lineage and does not invent a regional attribution.

## Product modes

- Classic solo puzzle
- Guided practice with a solved-state hint
- Reset and replay
- Responsive four-pit arctic board

No wallet, room, escrow or token mechanics are added because they do not improve this solo puzzle.
