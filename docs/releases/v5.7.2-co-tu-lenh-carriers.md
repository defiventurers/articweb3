## Confirmed implementation defects and resulting behavior

| Failure in 5.7.1 | Change in this APK |
|---|---|
| Only the top sprite was selectable | Stack panel selects the carrier or any passenger, including nested cargo. |
| Every action passed the turn immediately | A bounded compound turn retains the original stack's participants; each component acts once. Unrelated pieces and reboarding loops are rejected. |
| No way to stop a partial stack sequence | End turn finishes the remaining sequence explicitly. |
| Boarding was restricted to adjacent pieces | Normal piece range is respected; either the passenger or carrier may travel to form a valid stack. Loading consumes its own turn, preserving the prior loading policy. |
| Loaded vehicles could not be transported | Legal three-piece nested groups travel together; combined capacities and unit restrictions are enforced. |
| Cargo could not be left behind | Normal travel has passenger checkboxes. Invalid ground/sea abandonment is refused. |
| Aircraft had no delivery-and-return action | Choose a returning drop; a loaded Tank's descendants travel with it. Compatible flat passengers may land as a Tank stack. Other components may then use their remaining movement. |
| A Navy ship could use only one weapon | One ship may resolve its ship attack and land attack once each, with server validation of each step. |
| Engineer transport depended on an ordinary reef | Loaded engineers ferry straight across the river; general loaded travel is refused. |
| Destroying HQ also killed its protected Commander | Weak HQ destruction leaves the Commander alive and detached, with the attacker remaining in place. |
| Heavy guns could not capture across ordinary river segments | Eligible capture crossings are allowed; empty travel retains crossing restrictions. |
| Missile Hero diagonal range grew too far | The heritage one-step diagonal grows by one; air-defence radius is unchanged. |
| Flight handling could remove then restore target cargo incorrectly | Interception resolves before target removal. Continuous-zone kamikaze, gaps, cargo loss and last-aircraft defeat are separately tested. |

The original 11×12 artwork geometry and optional Red/Blue coverage controls are preserved. Coverage and flight interception still use the same defence calculation. No images were regenerated.
