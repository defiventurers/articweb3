# Phase 2 — replayable discoveries and a short expedition

Completed guided activities save compact replay descriptors and ordered legal action IDs. Replay reconstructs every frame from the canonical rules. It offers previous/next, replay and a playable retry. Memories sync as whole immutable records; action arrays never undergo set merging.

Archive → Replay my discoveries opens the collection. Share explicitly creates a public challenge containing a curated request, title, rules version and integrity digest. It contains no name, account ID, room, seat token, chat or solution path. A recovery account is needed to publish; recipients can solve without sign-up. Server workers recheck both publication and completion against the canonical lesson, and reject incompatible rules versions. Deep links work through `arcticplay://challenge/<id>`.

These are verified guided discoveries, not claims of verified competitive match victories. Full match analysis, player-authored puzzle editing, clubs and ratings remain later roadmap work.

Camp → The Sleeping Han Court has three board activities: a leaping Horse, Han activation and royal safety. Red/Shu remains at the bottom. Finishing lights a permanent Camp lantern and offers practice. It does not change the real game's first-turn order.
