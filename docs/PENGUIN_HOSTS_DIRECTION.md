## Safe integration rule

> The penguins are **friendly hosts around the game**, not historical game pieces. They may appear in catalogue covers, entry panels, Field guides, How to Play, practice nudges, result-side decoration, and non-interactive mode labels. They must not replace board animals, counters, cards, tokens, cultural imagery, or legal-state indicators.

## Shared visual behaviour

Each lead host appears as a compact, non-interactive mascot seal on a cover, entry surface, or open Field guide. The supporting host may appear in a smaller companion marker. Their colour becomes a secondary accent only; game-specific board colours and source-native pieces keep priority. Motion is limited to a subtle two-pixel bob outside gameplay and is removed when the user prefers reduced motion.
