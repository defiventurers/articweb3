## Sources and interpretation

- Shogi: the [Japan Shogi Association](https://www.shogi.or.jp/history/story/) describes 11th-century evidence and the later development of hon-shogi. [Shogi.cz](https://shogi.cz/en/about-us/) explains the English name.
- Sannin Shogi: the [history and references](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sannin_shogi) give an approximate 1930 creation and a recorded game in 1932. [BoardGameGeek’s catalogue](https://boardgamegeek.com/boardgame/70142/kokusai-sannin-shogi) also dates it to 1930.
- Sanguo Qi: [historical summaries and references](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Game_of_the_Three_Kingdoms) disagree between Southern Song and Qing origins. The card preserves that uncertainty instead of assigning a single year.
- San You Qi: [Cazaux’s historical research](https://history.chess.free.fr/sanguoqi.htm) and the [Three Friends history](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Game_of_the_Three_Friends) attribute it to Zheng Jinde in the Kangxi era, 1661–1722.
- Sanguo Yan Yi Qi: the [2005 design record](https://patents.google.com/patent/CN2870925Y/zh) documents the design on 22 August 2005. This is evidence of a documented design, not proof of the exact first day it was invented.
- Xiangqi: [Jim Png’s discussion of historical manuals](https://www.xiangqi.com/articles/a-brief-history-of-the-xiangqi-chinese-chess-opening) places the current form in the Song dynasty, 960–1279. [Etymology and references](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Xiangqi) explain the literal reading.
- Chaturanga: the [Japan Shogi Association](https://www.shogi.or.jp/history/world/chaturanga.php) distinguishes its traditions; [early-history references](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chaturanga) and the [Ludii evidence database](https://ludii.games/data.php?gameId=1292) separate early chess history from later four-player evidence. The app’s existing dice and team rules remain a modern edition.

Research checked 22 October 2026. App metadata contains concise paraphrases and source links, not copied source passages.

All Games sorts by earliest dated roots: Chaturanga, Xiangqi, Shogi, Sanguo Qi, San You Qi, Sannin Shogi, Sanguo Yan Yi Qi. Sanguo Qi uses the beginning of its earliest proposed range and retains the disputed-date label. These ordering anchors are not exact invention years.
