- Hall of the Week is an offline editorial rotation with a real rule challenge; it never implies a scheduled event or live attendance. Aurora Nights stays a future concept until real scheduling exists. No artificial countdowns or limited game access.
- Personalization remains optional and free discovery stays free. Existing inactive sales/chain integrations remain inactive.
- Acceptance evidence must distinguish automated browser and signature/package checks from physical-device install, TalkBack and live backend checks that were actually performed.
