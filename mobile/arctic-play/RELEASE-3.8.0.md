# Antarctic Kingdoms v3.8.0 — A celebration at every table

Creative and implementation record · 22 October 2026

The Eternal Gathering celebrates remarkable games and the people who enjoy them.
The current creative direction supersedes earlier revival wording and the old
“lost wisdom” motto. New Home motto: **Remarkable games. An endless celebration.**
World line: **Where remarkable games are celebrated.** The Antarctic society is
fiction; each game's existing origin and selected-edition label stays visible.
