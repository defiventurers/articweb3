# Heritage Catalogue Visual Refresh

## Three possible approaches

| Theme Name | Very Brief Intro | Probability |
|---|---|---:|
| **Frozen Printworks** | A scholarly letterpress archive: paper grain, restrained inks, and illustrated specimen labels. It feels collected and calm rather than game-like. | 0.04 |
| **Tactile Expedition Theatre** | A cinematic tabletop journey where each game arrives as a bright, physical play-kit under a moving polar sky. It is warm, playful, and instantly legible. | 0.08 |
| **Aurora Signal Room** | A dim control-room aesthetic that uses luminous coordinates and soft technical panels. It is sleek but risks feeling too electronic for the heritage focus. | 0.03 |

## Chosen approach: Tactile Expedition Theatre

### Design Movement

The catalogue follows **contemporary board-game editorial design** with a light influence from expedition journals and painted tabletop dioramas. It should feel like opening a carefully made modern game box, not using a generic game dashboard.

### Core Principles

1. **Every game is an artefact with a stage.** Covers use a single decisive object, route, or board topology, with room for the title to breathe.
2. **Tactics stay readable.** Gameplay surfaces remain high contrast and never use decoration that competes with legal moves, counters, or board paths.
3. **Delight is earned at transitions, not forced.** Brief tactile motion confirms an action; there are no streaks, scarcity, forced timers, or artificial rewards.
4. **A coherent cast guides the journey.** Auri, Cori, Mica, and Pip enrich entry and learning moments without replacing source-native animals or historic roles.

### Color Philosophy

The base is deep midnight blue and frost-white, allowing each game to own one warm or luminous expedition accent. Accents are used in small, intentional places—cover linework, active controls, and route signals—so that the collection feels varied but remains one archive.

### Layout Paradigm

Use an **asymmetric theatre** rather than a central card grid: a large illustrated cover or board sits slightly off-axis, while a compact field-note panel carries title, heritage, player count, and a clear action. On mobile, the cover becomes the first act and information follows as a readable playbill.

### Signature Elements

1. A cover-frame system with a die-cut window, foil-like accent line, archival game number, and one tactile visual motif per game.
2. A small **Expedition Brief** ribbon that explains the challenge and first move in a sentence.
3. Soft tabletop shadows, paper-fibre grain, and restrained snow/sparkle atmospheres that never cover controls.

### Interaction Philosophy

Selecting a game should feel like lifting a box from a shelf: the active cover rises a few pixels, the accent line brightens, and the related field note changes in place. Controls use direct language—**Enter board**, **Study the route**, **Play locally**—and always expose a calm exit or return path.

### Animation

Motion uses transform and opacity only, is limited to 160–420 ms, and respects `prefers-reduced-motion`. Covers receive a very slow environmental drift; buttons get a 0.97 active press; route ornaments reveal once on entry. Board-cell wrappers, hit targets, and game counters receive no structural animation or stacking changes.

### Typography System

Use **Bebas Neue** or the existing condensed display face for cover titles, **Rajdhani** for game controls and metadata, **DM Mono** for archival notation, and Georgia only for short heritage descriptions. Titles are compact, uppercase, and intentionally irregular in line breaks; body copy is calm and sentence-led.

### Brand Essence

**Arctic Game Kingdoms is a playable expedition through heritage board games, making distinct rulesets feel tactile, friendly, and worth returning to.**

Its personality is **curious, handcrafted, and clear-eyed**.

### Brand Voice

Headlines describe the immediate tactical promise; CTAs invite a real next move rather than manufacturing urgency. Example lines: **“Trace the outer route. Keep one move in reserve.”** and **“A small board. A sharp blockade.”** Generic greetings and empty “get started” language are excluded.

### Wordmark and Logo

The archive mark is a four-point ice compass carrying a tiny game node at its centre. The wordmark uses compressed, slightly weathered display lettering with a thin horizontal route line extending from the last character; it is never treated as a default-font label.

### Signature Brand Color

**Lantern Coral — `#FF7A69`** — is the cross-catalogue signal colour for active invitation and warm human guidance. Individual games retain their own accents around it.
